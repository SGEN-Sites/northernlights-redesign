Inside Northern Lights Whitehall

A jam-band living room in a village general store.

A Jerry Garcia portrait looks over a guitar on its stand. There’s a Persian rug, a leather armchair, live-edge wood and houseplants, and the staff remember regulars by name.

  • No attitude, LGBTQ+ friendly. Locals, lake-goers and Vermont neighbors take a seat and leave with exactly what they came for.
  • Music and pop-up nights. In the store’s own words: “great vibes, occasional music and Brand Pop-Up events!”
  • Quick-witted budtenders who help newcomers find their way. Ask anything.
  • The pizza place downstairs makes it a proper neighborhood hangout.

About Northern Lights

Family-owned, in Whitehall and Menands.

Northern Lights is a family-owned and operated adult-use cannabis retailer serving communities across New York. We’re committed to creating a welcoming, professional environment where adults 21 and over can explore carefully selected cannabis products with confidence.

Our approach is simple: offer fair pricing, reliable service, and a thoughtful customer experience. We proudly match any local price to ensure our customers receive consistent value, and we provide convenient delivery options to meet you wherever you are.

At Northern Lights, we focus on quality, transparency, and community—helping you make informed choices in a space built on trust and respect.

Our story

Good questions

What do I need to bring?

A valid government-issued photo ID. You must be 21 or older to shop, in store and for delivery.

What are your hours?

Both stores are open every day, 10am–8pm. Same-day delivery runs 11am–7pm.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Same-day delivery from Whitehall and Menands, free per each store’s menu. Order on the live menu and check your address at checkout. How delivery works.

How can I pay?

Whitehall: Pay by Phone, Debit Card, Pay by Bank or Cash. Menands: Debit Card, Pay by Bank or Cash.

Do you really price match?

Yes: “We price match guarantee with ANY recreational dispensary in the Capitol Region!” Ask us at the counter or call the store. Deals & price match.

How do Rewards work?

Join for 50 points. Earn 1 to 1.75 points per $1 by tier; 100 points take $5 off a $6+ purchase and 250 points take $15 off $16+. See the tiers.
