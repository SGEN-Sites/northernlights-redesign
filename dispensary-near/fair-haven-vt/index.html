Fair Haven to Whitehall

A dispensary near Fair Haven, VT

Follow US 4 west across the state line to Whitehall, New York, home of Northern Lights Whitehall. Buy here, enjoy it here, and leave it on the New York side.

Shop WhitehallThe Whitehall store

From Fair Haven, US 4 runs west across the state line into Whitehall, home of the closer of our two stores and the one Vermont neighbors already stop in at. It sits at the south tip of Lake Champlain, in a bright red clapboard building on Broadway with a pizza place downstairs. You can’t miss the red.

Keep it in New York

Buy and enjoy in New York. Never carry cannabis back across the state line into Vermont; that’s federal law. New York’s adult-use guide has the details.

Inside the red building

It’s a jam-band living room in a village general store: a Jerry Garcia portrait over a guitar on its stand, a leather armchair, a Persian rug, live-edge wood and houseplants. The staff are quick-witted, remember regulars by name and help newcomers find their way. No attitude, LGBTQ+-friendly, and a little seasonal humor to get you through mud season.

How Vermont neighbors shop with us

  • Order on the Whitehall live menu and pick up in store.
  • Walk in any day, 10am–8pm.
  • Staying on the New York side? Free same-day delivery runs 11am–7pm; check the address at checkout.

Our price match covers any recreational dispensary in the Capital Region, and Rewards starts you at 50 points. Whitehall takes Pay by Phone, Debit Card, Pay by Bank and Cash.

How do I get to Whitehall from Fair Haven?

Take US 4 west across the state line into Whitehall, then find Broadway. We’re at 132 Broadway.

What ID should I bring?

A valid government-issued photo ID that shows you’re 21 or older. See New York’s adult-use guide.

Can I take my purchase home to Vermont?

No. Don’t carry cannabis across the state line; it’s against federal law. Buy and enjoy in New York (state info).
