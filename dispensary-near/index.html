Towns closer to our Whitehall store

Whitehall sits where US 4 and NY 22 come through town at the south tip of Lake Champlain, so it’s the natural stop for these towns, from the Glens Falls area to Ticonderoga and across the line in Vermont.

  • Granville, NY
  • Fort Ann, NY
  • Hudson Falls, NY
  • Glens Falls, NY
  • Lake George, NY
  • Ticonderoga, NY
  • Fair Haven, VT
  • Rutland, VT

Coming from Vermont?

Buy and enjoy in New York, and never carry cannabis back across the state line; it’s federal law. See New York’s adult-use guide.

Towns closer to our Menands store

Menands is on Broadway (NY 32) just off I-787, near Albany and across the Troy–Menands Bridge from Troy, so it’s the easy pick for these Capital Region towns.

  • Albany, NY
  • Troy, NY
  • Watervliet, NY
  • Cohoes, NY
  • Colonie, NY
  • Latham, NY
  • Rensselaer, NY
  • Schenectady, NY

Wherever you’re coming from

Both stores are open every day, 10am–8pm, and both offer free same-day delivery from 11am to 7pm; check your address at checkout. Want the full picture first? See our two locations.
